BBCO Vault privacy policy

Big Bus Co · last updated 13 November 2026

What the extension does

BBCO Vault for Chrome offers to save the passwords and passkeys you choose into the BBCO Vault app on your own Windows computer.

What it collects

Only what you choose to save: the website address, the username and the password, or a passkey made for that website. Nothing is collected unless you press Save.

Where it goes

Straight to the BBCO Vault app on the same computer, over Chrome's local native-messaging channel. It is stored there in one file encrypted with AES-256, opened only with your master password or your recovery code.

The extension and the app send nothing to Big Bus Co or to any server. There is no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

What it keeps in Chrome

Only the list of websites where you chose "Never for this site". A login you are asked about is held in memory for up to two minutes, then forgotten.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Removing it

Remove the extension in chrome://extensions. Your vault stays on your computer until you delete it.
